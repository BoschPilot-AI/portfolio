Common Questions

FAQ

Everything you need to know before we work together.

What tools do you work with?
Primarily n8n, Make.com, and GoHighLevel — but I can integrate with most modern business tools including HubSpot, Airtable, Google Workspace, OpenAI, and more. If it has an API, it can be connected.
How long does a project take?
Simple automations take 3–7 days. Complex multi-platform systems like Bruce or the ASRG pipeline typically take 2–4 weeks depending on scope and client feedback cycles.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?
No. I handle all the technical setup. I just need to understand your current process and what you want to happen automatically. If you can describe it, I can build it.
What happens after the system is built?
I provide full documentation and a walkthrough so your team can manage it confidently. I also offer ongoing support packages if you need changes or additions down the line.
Can you build something specific to my industry?
Yes — I've built systems for healthcare clinics, real estate, cybersecurity, tax services, and marketing agencies. If your business has repetitive operations, there's almost always a way to automate them.
